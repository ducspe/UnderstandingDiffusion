This workshop explores diffusion models from several angles, connecting their mathematical foundations with practical algorithms to build intuition for how and why they work.

The presentation is available as [PowerPoint](UnderstandingDiffusion_BehindTheScenesTheory_September2026Version_Final.pptx) and [PDF](UnderstandingDiffusion_BehindTheScenesTheory_September2026Version_Final.pdf) and covers 3 chapters:

1. **Defining Flow and Diffusion Models Through the Lens of Differential Equations:** Introduces flow models as ordinary differential equations (ODEs) and diffusion models as stochastic differential equations (SDEs).
2. **From Conditional to Marginal: Single Data Points to the Full Distribution:** Explains how probability paths, vector fields, and score functions for individual data points relate to the full data distribution.
3. **Practical Training Algorithms:** Covers conditional flow matching and denoising score matching, their training objectives, and how trained models generate samples.
